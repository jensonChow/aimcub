Add examples/eval-moat: a reproducible benchmark for the v1 beta gate "context and personalized eval demonstrably improve later decompositions". Three committed personas seed two conditions of the same aim — a bare first-run store and one with context accrued the product's own way (prior aims proven by evidence, candidates reviewed, some rows deliberately withheld) — then decompose both through the real pipeline and score the plans blind against a fixed five-criterion rubric, with a seeded A/B order and a deterministic `critiquePlan` cross-check. Dry run is the default and never contacts a provider; live mode announces its call estimate and aborts at a hard cap. The report can state a null or negative result, and the harness's own findings (unrelated-aim context admitted on function-word overlap; the deterministic scorer penalising honestly human-routed plans) are surfaced in the output rather than smoothed over.
